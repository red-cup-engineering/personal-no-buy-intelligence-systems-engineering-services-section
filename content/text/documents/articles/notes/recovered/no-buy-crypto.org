#+title: No-Buy Crypto

There is a new economy forming out of blockchain technologies. Whether this economy exists within, alongside, or outside of the existing economy is irrelevant to the choice of whether to start participating in this new tokenized economy, today.

Institutional investors have established a presence in the tokenized economy, and its being used at a global industrial scale for human necessities like healthcare allocation. I don't see it as a choice of whether we participate in the token economy or not: it's a matter of whether we choose to start doing it now, before the economy is put upon us by externalities.

/(This mimicks my view toward things like exiting resource overshoot: I do not think it is a choice of/ whether /we stop using more resources than are sustainable, but when. Early adopters tend to be those who thrive under changed circumstances. I prefer to thrive, so I choose to do things like work toward exiting resource overshoot, or entering the token economy, today. To me, this is a big part of living as an anarchist: recognizing I get to choose how I act, and so, making those choices.)/
